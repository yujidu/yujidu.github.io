Our paper *"Thermo-hydro-mechanical coupled material point method for modeling freezing and thawing of porous media"*, published in the *International Journal for Numerical and Analytical Methods in Geomechanics* (IJNAMG), has been recognized by Wiley as a **Top Viewed Article**, ranking among the top 10% most-viewed papers published in the journal in 2024.

The article has received more than 3,400 views to date. Earlier this year, the same work was also named one of the journal's top 10 most-cited articles. We are delighted to see the research attracting such strong interest from the community.
