On 1 November 2024, Jidu began his visit as a Visiting Ph.D. Researcher in [Prof. Kenichi Soga](https://geomechanics.berkeley.edu/people/soga/)'s group in the Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering at UC Berkeley. The visit is supported by the HKUST Overseas Research Award.

Jidu is grateful to his supervisor, [Prof. Jidong Zhao](https://jzhao.people.ust.hk/), for this opportunity and for his strong recommendation, and to Prof. Soga for kindly hosting the visit.

He looks forward to an enjoyable and productive stay in Berkeley.
