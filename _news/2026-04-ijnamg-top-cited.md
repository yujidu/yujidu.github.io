We are pleased to share that our paper *"Thermo-hydro-mechanical coupled material point method for modeling freezing and thawing of porous media"*, published in the *International Journal for Numerical and Analytical Methods in Geomechanics* (IJNAMG), has been recognized by Wiley as a **Top Cited Article**, ranking among the journal's top 10 most-cited articles published in 2024.

Developed as part of Jidu's Ph.D. research at HKUST, the work introduced a thermo-hydro-mechanical (THM) coupled material point method (MPM) for freezing and thawing porous media. To our knowledge, it is among the earliest fully coupled THM modeling frameworks capable of capturing thawing-induced large deformation.

Jidu is grateful to his co-authors: his Ph.D. supervisor, [Prof. Jidong Zhao](https://jzhao.people.ust.hk/), and his HKUST colleagues Dr. Shiwei Zhao and Dr. Weijian Liang, for their support throughout this work.
