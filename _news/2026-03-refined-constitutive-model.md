Widely graded granular soils change their gradation as particles break, and this shifts their critical state.

Building on breakage tests with artificially dyed particles, this collaborative work proposes a fractional breakage evolution model, a gradation-dependent critical state line, and a state-dependent dilatancy relation based on fractional-order plasticity. Together they form a refined constitutive model that reproduces triaxial tests on five granular soils under different loading conditions.
