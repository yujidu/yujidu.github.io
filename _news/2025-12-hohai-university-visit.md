On 15 December 2025, Jidu visited his master's supervisor, [Prof. Sihong Liu](https://sdxy.hhu.edu.cn/2021/0926/c15302a228739/page.htm), at Hohai University in Nanjing. At Prof. Liu's invitation, he shared his recent research and reflections on his academic journey with the faculty and students of the research group.

Afterwards, everyone had lunch together. Jidu sincerely thanks Prof. Liu for his warm hospitality. Every return to Hohai feels like coming home.
