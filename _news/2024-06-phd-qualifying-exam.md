On 20 June 2024, Jidu passed his Ph.D. Qualifying Examination at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST).

The examination committee consisted of Prof. Gang Wang, Dr. Shiwei Zhao and his supervisor, [Prof. Jidong Zhao](https://jzhao.people.ust.hk/). Jidu sincerely thanks the committee members for their time, insightful questions and valuable suggestions on his research.
