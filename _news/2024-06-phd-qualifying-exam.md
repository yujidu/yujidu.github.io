On 20 June 2024, Jidu passed his Ph.D. Qualifying Examination at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), presenting his research proposal titled *"Multiphysics and Multiscale Modeling of Phase Transition and Large Deformation in Granular Media"*.

The examination committee consisted of Prof. Gang Wang, his co-supervisor Dr. Shiwei Zhao, and his supervisor [Prof. Jidong Zhao](https://jzhao.people.ust.hk/). Jidu sincerely thanks the committee members for their time, insightful questions and valuable suggestions on his research.
