From 21 to 26 July 2024, Jidu attended the 16th World Congress on Computational Mechanics and 4th Pan American Congress on Computational Mechanics (WCCM-PANACM 2024), held at the Vancouver Convention Centre in Vancouver, Canada.

He gave a talk titled *"Thermo-hydro-mechanical coupled material point method for simulating the freeze-thaw behavior of porous media"*, joint work with Prof. Jidong Zhao, Dr. Shiwei Zhao and Dr. Weijian Liang, presenting a THM-coupled MPM framework for modeling phase transition and large deformation in freezing and thawing soils.
