\section{Research Interests}
\vspace{-1pt}
\cvrow{}{My research develops high-fidelity computational methods for geomaterials in extreme environments,
where coupled thermal, hydraulic, mechanical and chemical (THMC) processes drive large deformation and failure.}
\vspace{-3pt}
\begin{cvbullets}
  \item Particle and hybrid numerical methods: MPM, DEM, MPM-DEM, MPM-FVM
  \item Multiphysics (THM/C) modelling of multiphase granular media, e.g., frozen soils and hydrate-bearing sediments
  \item Climate-driven geohazards: permafrost thaw, rainfall-induced landslides, hydrate dissociation-induced instability
  \item Geomechanics in extreme environments: submarine, deep underground and extraterrestrial
  \item Machine learning-aided multiscale and multiphysics modelling
  \item 3D printing of extrusion-based materials: rheology and numerical modelling
\end{cvbullets}
